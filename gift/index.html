Cover LMK for your student.

They'll know what to do today — and what it's going to take — without you having to ask.

One payment. No renewal, no card kept on file, nothing to cancel. When it ends they simply go back to the free version with everything they've built still there.

How it works

  1. Ask your student for their LMK code — eight characters, in the app under LMK plans. They can send it to you in a text.
  2. Pick a length above and pay. You'll be asked for that code at the checkout.
  3. That's it. It lands on their account within seconds. Nothing for them to set up, nothing to redeem, and no account for you to create.

What they get

  • A daily plan built from their real Canvas assignments, sized to the hours they actually have that day.
  • A tutor that has already read the assignment and the marking rubric before they ask it anything.
  • They can photograph a problem set, or upload the lecture slides and ask about those.
  • Thirty questions a day. The free version is twenty in total, once — not per day — so this is the difference between trying it and using it.
  • Longer quizzes and bigger flashcard decks when they're revising.
  • You will never see their grades, their work, or what they do in the app. This is a gift, not a monitor — there is no parent dashboard and there will not be one.
  • What LMK itself handles is written down plainly in the privacy policy, including the small brief the tutor is given when they ask it something.
