Your data is yours

Everything LMK stores about you lives in your own browser and, if you turn on sync, in a file in your own Google Drive. LMK has no copy. How data is handled is described in full in the privacy policy, which is part of these terms.

Your part

  • Don't try to break it, overload it, or use it to collect other people's information.
  • If you see something wrong, tell us. That is genuinely the most useful thing you can do.
