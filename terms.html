LMK — Terms of Use

Effective September 10, 2026 · Applies to the LMK app at lmktoday.app and the "LMK for Canvas" browser extension

LMK is a planner that reads your Canvas assignments and tells you what to do today. These terms are short because the product is simple. Using LMK means you agree to them.

What LMK is, and isn't

  • LMK's planner is free to use and always will be: the plan, the estimates, the extension and the sync cost nothing. The AI tutor is metered, and there are paid plans for more of it, described below. You are never charged without choosing a plan in the app first.
  • There is no sign-up, no email and no password. The app makes a random id for your device so a plan and a purchase can follow you between devices; it is not tied to your name.
  • LMK's time estimates, start dates, grade calculations and "what to do today" are estimates and suggestions. Your syllabus, your professor's gradebook and Canvas itself are the official record. When they disagree with LMK, they win.
  • LMK never submits work, changes anything in Canvas, or acts on your behalf. It only reads.
  • LMK is not affiliated with Arizona State University, Instructure, or any school. It is built and run by one student.

How old you have to be

LMK is for students 13 and over. It is not built for children under 13 and you should not use it if you are.

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian has to read these terms and agree to them for you, and they have to be the one who buys anything — a paid plan or a pack of units. Do not enter card details without them. Everything free stays free either way: the plan, the estimates, the extension, the sync and reviewing your flashcards never ask for a card.

LMK works with any school whose Canvas runs on an instructure.com address, including high schools; other Canvas addresses can be added by hand with a Canvas access token, and the extension is learning to add them itself. If you are using a laptop your school owns and manages, your school decides what you may install on it — LMK cannot change that, and you should not try to get around it.

Your data is yours

Everything LMK stores about you lives in your own browser and, if you turn on sync, in a file in your own Google Drive. A few things live on LMK's server, and each is named here. When you finish the welcome screen, LMK makes a random id for this device and keeps it with a first-seen date, so a plan you buy can follow you to another device; it carries no name or email. If you use Sync from anywhere — offered as a ticked box on the welcome screen, so it is on unless you untick it, and it can be switched off under the gear at any time — the server keeps your Canvas access key encrypted, and a plain copy of your assignment list, scores included, so your phone stays current while your laptop is closed. If you use the tutor, a hashed form of your network address is kept for a day to spread the free allowance fairly, and if the app crashes it counts the crash. Stripe keeps your card and email; LMK never sees them.

Your part

  • Use LMK with your own Canvas account only.
  • Don't try to break it, overload it, or use it to collect other people's information.
  • If you see something wrong, tell us. That is genuinely the most useful thing you can do.

Classmates. If you join a class's list of classmates, the name and contact line you show are yours to choose and yours to remove; use a name people in that class would recognise, put nothing there you would not say in the class group chat, and treat a study session like any plan with people you have not met: a library, a campus spot, or a call. LMK confirms that everyone shown is enrolled in the class through Canvas, and nothing more. Anyone can report an entry; an entry that is abusive, impersonates someone, or is used to harass a classmate is removed and the account behind it loses the feature. Tutor units added for inviting a classmate are a one-time trial top-up, not a payment, and can be withdrawn if invites are faked.

LMK Plus

LMK has a free plan and paid plans. Each plan sets how much of the tutor you can use in a day, measured in units: one message to the tutor is a unit; a quiz or a deck of flashcards costs five, because it produces far more; deep thinking costs twelve, because it runs on a larger model; a question about a photo costs two and one about an uploaded file three. Reviewing cards you have already made is free and unlimited on every plan. Units can also be bought in packs, once, without a subscription; bought units do not expire and are used only after a day's included units are gone. The plans, their prices and their limits are shown in the app before you pay and can change; the app always shows the current ones.

LMK Plus is the paid tier. Everything the free version does stays free forever: the plan, the estimates, the extension, the sync, and reviewing every flashcard you make. The AI tutor is different, because it costs real money each time it answers — a free account gets a one-off trial of it, not a daily allowance, and once the trial is spent it does not refill. Plus and Pro give the tutor back, every day. The price is shown in the app before you pay, billing is handled by Stripe, and you can cancel any time from the app; access runs to the end of the period you paid for. Moving between paid plans is handled by Stripe's billing portal, which credits the unused part of the period you already paid for against the new plan. Every account starts on the free plan. LMK may grant Plus to an account by hand, for example to a tester or a class, for a stated number of months; that is a gift, not a right, and it ends when the months do.

The tutor costs real money every time it answers, so its use is metered in units. A free account gets a fixed number of units once — a trial, which does not come back when it is spent. A paid plan gets a number of units every day, resetting at midnight UTC. Packs of units can be bought on any plan at the same price for everyone, and bought units never expire. A message costs one unit; a quiz or a deck of flashcards costs five, because it produces far more. The current numbers are always shown in the app before you pay. The free "Study with AI" buttons, which hand a prepared prompt to the AI you already use, stay free and unlimited.

No warranty, plainly

LMK is provided as is. It is built carefully and tested, but it can be wrong, late, or unavailable, and Canvas can change underneath it. LMK is not responsible for a missed deadline, a grade, or any other consequence of relying on it. Keep checking Canvas for anything that matters.
