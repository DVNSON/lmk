Canvas is Canvas — the address is what differs.

The extension reads canvas.asu.edu and any instructure.com address out of the box. Most universities run Canvas on their own address, and LMK reads those too once you add the address under the gear. These are the schools whose Canvas we have checked.

  • ASUcanvas.asu.edu · out of the box
  • UWcanvas.uw.edu · add under the gear
  • UCLAbruinlearn.ucla.edu · add under the gear
  • Harvardcanvas.harvard.edu · add under the gear
  • UW–Madisoncanvas.wisc.edu · add under the gear
  • UC Daviscanvas.ucdavis.edu · add under the gear
  • Penncanvas.upenn.edu · add under the gear
  • Rutgerscanvas.rutgers.edu · add under the gear
  • UT Dallaselearning.utdallas.edu · add under the gear

Your school isn't here?

If your Canvas address ends in instructure.com, nothing to add — install and open Canvas once. Otherwise install, open LMK, and choose Add my school's Canvas under the gear; or use the Canvas access-token route there, which works on any school and on a phone. Brightspace schools work the same way through brightspace.com.
