Let Canvas do the tracking

Laptop: install the LMK for Canvas extension (the lmk-extension folder → chrome://extensions → Load unpacked). Open Canvas once and LMK learns every assignment, what's already submitted, your scores, and which items are in-class or not graded. Phone: tap the LMK sync bookmark while on Canvas, then paste what it copied into the box below — no course needed.

Or paste a page

Open a course in Canvas → Assignments → select the whole page (Ctrl/Cmd-A), copy, paste it here.

Cloud sync (cross-device): when you open this app on claude.ai with the Google Drive connector added (claude.ai Settings → Connectors), your checkmarks, notes, syllabi, and added assignments save to a private LMK-data.json in your own Google Drive and follow you across devices. Everyone who uses the app syncs to their own Drive — nobody can see anyone else's data. No Drive? Everything still works, saved on this device.

Auto-sync: the built-in ASU semester re-pulls itself from Canvas every morning — new assignments and changed due dates just show up. An uploaded calendar is a snapshot: re-upload any time to refresh it. The + button up top covers work that isn’t in Canvas at all.

Heads up: your calendar feed link contains a private token — treat it like a password and don’t post it anywhere. This app never sends your link or your data off this device.
